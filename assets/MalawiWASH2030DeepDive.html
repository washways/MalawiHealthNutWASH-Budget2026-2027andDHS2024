WASH 2030 Deep Dive

Malawi WASH financing, staffing, utility performance, and district delivery readiness to 2030.

This page is purely WASH-focused. It consolidates visible budget lines from MOAIWD, MOHS, NWRA, waterboards, and CDF, then links them to staffing constraints, district opportunity patterns, and financing scenarios against SDG 6 and Malawi's Climate-Resilient WASH financing pathway.
